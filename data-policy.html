Data Policy

What we store on this community server, why we store it, and how it relates to research and backups.

Website forum accounts

The site forum uses Google sign-in. We store your Google subject ID, email, Google display name, chosen username, privacy flags (whether email is public; whether Google name is hidden), session records, and the threads and posts you create. Defaults: email is private; showing a username instead of your Google name is optional.

Gameplay & account data

We store Minecraft usernames, UUIDs (and Floodgate Bedrock IDs if used), permission data, homes, kits, inventories, and similar plugin state needed for the world to work.

Player actions & research

Player actions — such as movement, builds, block changes, chat, and other in-game activity — may be recorded to help run the server and to build an AI research dataset for the owner’s academic / personal research. This is not sold as a product. Joining the server means you understand that gameplay activity may be logged for these purposes.

Logs & moderation

Server and plugin logs may include join/leave events, chat, commands, and IPs when logging is enabled (log-ips). CoreProtect and similar tools record block changes for rollback and abuse investigation.

Backups & availability

We take backups of the world and related data. The server is meant to stay active as long as there is interest from the community.

Home-lab context

This server also helps the owner test home-server capacity (CPU, RAM, network, and storage under real multiplayer load). Operational metrics may be observed for that purpose.

What we don’t do

We do not sell player data. Access is limited to operators who need it to run, protect, or research the world.
