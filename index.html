Server rules

Leave personal vendettas and politics outside — we just wanna play Minecraft. Break these and you may be kicked or banned.

  1. 01
    No griefing

    Don’t destroy or steal builds without clear permission.

  2. 02
    No cheats

    X-ray, fly hacks, and combat macros are out. Performance mods are fine.

  3. 03
    Keep it chill

    No harassment or hate speech. Leave personal vendettas and politics outside the server.

  4. 04
    All-ages community

    18+ content and interactions are discouraged. This server is open to all ages — keep chat and builds appropriate.

  5. 05
    Don’t crash the server

    No lag machines, item duplication, or mass redstone abuse.

From the owner

A note about this server

This world exists for fun. Everything here is 100% free — no pay-to-win, no ranks for sale, no pressure to spend.

I’m also using it to test my home server’s capacity and to record player actions as an AI research dataset. That means builds, movement, and in-game activity may be logged so I can study gameplay patterns later. Details live in the Data Policy and Privacy Policy.

We take backups. The server stays online as long as there’s interest — when people still want to play, the world keeps going.

Thanks for being here. Let’s just play Minecraft.
