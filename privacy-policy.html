Privacy Policy

How we treat information connected to your play on this free community server.

Who we are

USC Minecraft server by Citadel Codex is an unofficial community server associated with players from the University of San Carlos circle. It is not affiliated with the University of San Carlos, Mojang Studios, or Microsoft.

Information we process

When you join the Minecraft server, we may process Minecraft identifiers (username, UUID), connection metadata (such as IP when logging is enabled), chat, commands, and in-game actions. See the Data Policy for the operational and research uses of that data.

When you use the website forum, we process your Google account identifiers (subject ID, email, and display name) via Google sign-in, a session cookie so you stay signed in, the username and privacy preferences you choose, and the posts you publish. Email is not shown publicly unless you opt in. You may hide your Google display name and use a community username instead.

Why we process it

  • To run multiplayer gameplay and keep the world consistent
  • To moderate grief, abuse, and rule-breaking
  • To maintain backups and restore the world if needed
  • To support the owner’s AI research dataset and home-server capacity testing
  • To operate the community forum (authentication, authorship, and moderation of posts)

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information stays with the operators unless we are required to disclose it by law, or you ask us to share something specific (for example, a build screenshot you want posted). Forum sign-in uses Google as the identity provider; Google receives the OAuth sign-in request according to their policies. Forum and account data are stored in our hosted database (Coolify / Postgres on our infrastructure).

Retention

Game world data and backups may be kept for as long as the server remains interesting to the community. Logs and research exports may be retained longer when needed for moderation or research integrity.

Your choices

Joining the Minecraft server is voluntary. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy or the Data Policy, do not unlock or use the server address. Forum use is also voluntary — you can browse without signing in; posting requires Google sign-in and a profile. You control whether your email is public and whether your Google name is hidden. For questions or deletion requests where feasible, get in touch using the contact details below.

Children & all ages

The server is intended as an all-ages space. 18+ content and interactions are discouraged. Guardians should decide whether Minecraft multiplayer is appropriate for their household.
